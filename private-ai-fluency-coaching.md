# Private AI Fluency Coaching

A few years ago, I was worried that AI would eliminate my business, a digital agency building and managing applications for our clients, but at the same time, I was fascinated by how AI works. I still have these conflicting feelings of fear and excitement but my observations in recent months have eased some of my fears. Because I can work so much faster with AI, I was concerned that I would run out of things to do, but that is not the case. The quality of what we can deliver as a small agency has exponentially increased. I used to look at apps like Google Maps and wished someone would pay us millions of dollars to build something like it. Well, we can now, because AI is amazing at execution. That’s what clients too are catching on to. Custom apps that they could not have dreamed of owning, now they can, with all the bells and whistles that they have come to expect from giants like Google.

So, don’t worry. If you use AI to do your work and show your boss how fast you can get things done, you will actually be busier. As they say: if you want something done, ask a busy person to do it. You will experience this busy-person phenomenon first hand. Your spreadsheet will not only have more intelligent analyses, more details, and charts, but also look beautiful because you will have time left to design it. It will look like a research report from McKinsey.

But first, you have to overcome your AI anxiety. Trust me. That’s pretty easy if you sit with me for an hour. I help people learn AI by using it on real problems from their own work and life. We sit together, side by side. You stay at the keyboard. I guide you through what to ask, how to ask it, how to judge the answer, what’s safe or risky, and how to keep going when the AI gets stuck or gets something wrong. This is not a lecture, a webinar, or a generic prompt list. It is practical, personal, hands-on coaching until AI starts to click.

Here are some examples of what I have done for my clients:

One client was highly skeptical of AI but was an enthusiast of smart home technology, so I pointed to the air conditioner that happened to be right behind us. “Let’s write an app that can control it.” So, we launched OpenAI’s Codex app and asked if it can scan the home network to find the AC. Sure enough, it found its IP address. We then asked it to query what can be controlled. The AC returned a list of things we can control. So, we asked Codex to write a little app to control those parameters. We then said to Codex: “Turn it on.” Voila! It came on. My client was completely sold.

One client came to me because he wanted me to build a website for him. But after hearing more about it, I told him that he can just ask AI to build it for him. He was not ready to believe it but I told him to take out his laptop, so I can get him started. First, I had him open a new account at Amazon Web Services (AWS). I told him what to say to the AI agent so that it would just spin up a web server in the cloud. It installed a database and a framework for building websites. In no time, the website he needed was done. The final touch was to point a domain name to it. Viola! He had the website he wanted me to build. He went home excited and kept adding more and more to it.

With a photography enthusiast, I demonstrated that he could write a plugin for Adobe Lightroom that would export his photos in the exact format for Instagram Story, branded specifically for him with additional information about the photos, like date, time, aperture and shutter speed.

We now live in the era where you do not have to share apps with other people to subsidize the cost of development. You can “fly private” and once you do, you won’t go back to flying commercial.

Part of my incentive for offering this service is that I want to know how AI can be used in different industries and professions. It is partly a research project. Depending on how serious you are, I can offer: two-hour breakthrough session to help you overcome your AI anxiety and go home feeling inspired. Half-day AI intensive where we use AI to do your real work. Full-day intensive where we also dive into how AI works and what it’s good for and what it’s not good for, so you would not waste your time trying to solve problems that AI is not meant for.
